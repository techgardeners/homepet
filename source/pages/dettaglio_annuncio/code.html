About Elena

Hi! I'm Elena. I've been a professional pet sitter for over 5 years. My home is a sanctuary for animals, featuring a large fenced backyard and plenty of sunny spots for afternoon naps. I grew up on a farm and have experience with animals of all sizes and temperaments.

I work from home as a freelance designer, which means your pet will never be left alone for long periods. We go for morning jogs at Ocean Beach and evening strolls through Golden Gate Park. Cuddles are mandatory and unlimited!
